Privacy Policy

SVG Viewer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or advertising.
  • Files you open are read locally and never leave your Mac. The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and only accesses files you open, drop, or save.
  • SVG files cannot load remote resources or run scripts while displayed in the app.
  • Preferences (such as your background choice) are stored locally in the app's own container.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties and nothing to request or delete.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
